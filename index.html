Weather Sites

National Weather Service Near-Shore Marine Forecast for our section of Lake Michigan Shoreline.
      Near-shore meaning within 5 nautical miles of shore. This is as opposed to the Open Waters Forecast.
Harrison-Dever Crib
     NOAA weather station 2.7 miles offshore at the Harrison-Dever crib complex. Extremely valuable go-to resource. Gives you everything but wave-height info, and records everything hourly so you can see the trends.
Chicago- Perdue Met Bouy
     A seasonal bouy on the water north of the Navy Pier breakwall. Wonderful on-the-water information.
