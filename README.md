## Weather
National Weather Service Near-Shore Marine Forecast for our section of Lake Michigan Shoreline.  Near-shore meaning within 5 nautical miles of shore. This is as opposed to the Open Waters Forecast.    
[LINK](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=41.9394841&lon=-87.6540902)

 NOAA weather station 2.7 miles offshore at the Harrison-Dever crib complex.  Extremely valuable go-to resource.  Gives you everything but wave-height info, and records everything hourly so you can see the trends.   [LINK](https://www.ndbc.noaa.gov/station_page.php?station=chii2)


[Harrison-Dever Crib](https://www.glerl.noaa.gov/metdata/chi/met1m.php) aka dual-crib off of Navy Pier.


[Chicago Bouy](https://iiseagrant.org/45198/) is a seasonal course of information.

[Sail Flow](https://www.sailflow.com/windlist/60614)
